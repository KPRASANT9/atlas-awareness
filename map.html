The world map

Each country is coloured by its national anthem's text. Equal Earth projection, so every country appears at its true relative size. Small states are drawn as circles.

Themes, not rankings: colours describe what the words talk about, not the worth of a country or its people. Less certain scores are drawn closer to the neutral "typical" colour.
